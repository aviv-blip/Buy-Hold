How you're buying it

hard money — Boomerang
Hard money is borrowed and then repaid by the refinance, so only its fees and interest reach your final number — what it really buys you is not having the full purchase price liquid. Paying cash skips those fees and lowers your basis, but ties up everything until the refinance.
0 = you fund rehab. Set 100 if Boomerang funds draws.
03

How you're refinancing

DSCR loan
Set the LTV and read the three numbers underneath: what the loan is, what comes back to you, and whether it qualifies.
A DSCR lender underwrites gross rent ÷ PITIA — principal, interest, tax, insurance, HOA. Management and reserves don't count against you for qualifying, so this ratio runs higher than your real coverage. Most want 1.20–1.25 and 3–6 months of seasoning.
